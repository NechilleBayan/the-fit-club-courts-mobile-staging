Status Legend

Every status is drawn as well as named, so the screen still reads correctly in bright sun, in greyscale, and to anyone who cannot separate the red from the ochre or either from the ink. There are two coloured families and they mean different things: Red is something wrong or lost, ochre is something that needs attention. Neither leans on its colour to say so. The two hues are nearly the same lightness, because both have to stay readable as small type on paper, so each carries a glyph and an edge of its own: A warning triangle and a solid border, or a flag and a thick leading one.

The words are the customer build's words. A booking a customer is told is Held is Held here too, and never Reserved: Held is the one that admits the time is not secured yet, which is the whole point of saying it. Needs review is the same state the customer sees when a payment arrives that does not match its order.

Solid
Available, Paid, Completed, Confirmed
Hatched
Held, Pending, Unpaid, In progress
Dotted
In use, Checked in, Session running
Dashed
Blocked, Closed, Cancelled, Refunded
Double
Maintenance, out of service
Alert
Overdue, Failed, Conflict, Needs review. Something is wrong or lost. A warning triangle and the red
Flagged
Shift unfilled, Unanswered, Almost full, and every pending count. Something needs attention. A flag, a thick leading edge, and the ochre
Filled
Full. Every seat sold, which is a fact and not a fault

Priority is not a status and does not use any of the eight above. A High task carries the filled chip; Medium and Low stay quiet.
