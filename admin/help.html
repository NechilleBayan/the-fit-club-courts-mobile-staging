Status Legend

Every status is drawn as well as named, so the screen still reads correctly in bright sun, in greyscale, and to anyone who cannot separate the red from the ink.

Solid
Available, Paid, Completed, Confirmed
Hatched
Reserved, Pending, Unpaid, In progress
Dotted
In use, Checked in, Session running
Dashed
Blocked, Closed, Cancelled, Refunded
Double
Maintenance, out of service
Red
Overdue, Failed, Conflict

Priority is not a status and does not use any of the six above. A High task carries the filled chip; Medium and Low stay quiet.
